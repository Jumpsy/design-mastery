A short history · scroll →
1886

Atlanta. Pharmacist John Stith Pemberton mixes the syrup. It’s first sold at Jacobs’ Pharmacy, five cents a glass.

1915

The contour bottle. The Root Glass Company of Terre Haute, Indiana designs a bottle you could recognise by touch in the dark.

1954

Everywhere. A print ad boasts that “in over 80 countries the Coca-Cola bottle is familiar.”

1985

New Coke. The formula changes on April 23. Fans revolt, and the original is back 79 days later as Coca-Cola Classic.

Today

200+ countries and territories. Same red, same contour, same cold click of the cap.
