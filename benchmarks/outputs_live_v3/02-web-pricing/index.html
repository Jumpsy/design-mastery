Can I change plans as my team grows?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge. Downgrades apply at your next renewal, so you keep the features you have already paid for until then.

What happens when I reach a project limit?

Archive a finished project to make room for a new one, or move to Studio. Your archived projects stay available to view and export.

Can I cancel a yearly plan?

Yes. Cancel renewal at any time and use your plan through the end of the paid year. Yearly payments are not refunded for unused months.
