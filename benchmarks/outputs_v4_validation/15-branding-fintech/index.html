Rules of Application

Anti-slop design constraints and usage rules.

A brand system is defined as much by what it prohibits as what it permits. These non-negotiable rules safeguard the brand from slipping into either legacy stodginess or generic startup slop.

01. No Decorative Gradients or Blobs

Never apply generic multi-color gradient sweeps, mesh blurs, or purple-to-blue SaaS blobs. Vectis surfaces must remain crisp, architectural, and grounded in solid Void Slate, Bone Canvas, or Pure Surface.

02. No Rounded Pill Capsules

Never use 50% or 999px fully-rounded pill capsules for buttons or containers. Pills evoke casual consumer toys; Vectis uses disciplined 4px to 6px architectural corner radii.

03. No Two Similar-Weight Sans Faces

Never pair Plus Jakarta Sans with another generic sans-serif (e.g. Inter or Roboto). High visual contrast between the editorial Newsreader display and the engineered sans body is mandatory.

04. No Uncalibrated Low-Contrast Grays

All body text pairings must exceed WCAG 2.1 AA (4.5:1). Core narrative text on Bone Canvas uses Void Slate (#0e131b) achieving a 17.19:1 ratio, ensuring effortless legibility in high-stress operational centers.
