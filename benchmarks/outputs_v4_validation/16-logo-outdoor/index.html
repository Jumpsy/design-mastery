  • Generic SaaS Minimalist Sans: All-lowercase geometric sans with wide tracking and no typographic point of view.

  • Concept: Geological volcanic terroir and aerodynamic fluid-bed convection roasting. Single-origin micro-lots from 1,800m+ elevation.
  • Typographic Architecture: Custom chiseled lapidary letterforms with 45° facet cuts, unified datum alignment, and balanced triangular counters.
  • Chromatic Contrast: Cold Basalt Obsidian (#0F1416), Chalk White (#F7F8F6), and high-temperature Thermal Vermilion (#E25C2B).
02 / Construction & Geometry

Typographic Anatomy & Optical Balance

Every glyph is crafted with intentional stroke contrast (3.4:1 vertical-to-horizontal ratio), geometric terminal cuts, and optical counter compensation.

Glyph 'S' — Sculptural Spine

Width 66u · Cap 100u

Sharp horizontal terminal cuts with acute chisel endpoints. The diagonal spine has calibrated stroke tension, inflecting across the central geological datum line.

Glyph 'T' — Cantilevered Roof

Width 72u · Cap 100u

Architectural crossbeam with 45° chiseled downward wedge facets. Grounded by bracketed Roman triangular foot serifs to establish vertical authority.

Glyph 'R' — Dynamic Strut

Width 78u · Cap 100u

Elevated circular bowl joining the vertical stem at the 58u waist. The diagonal leg extends with an architectural flare, terminating with a chisel cut at the baseline.

Glyph 'A' — Summit Apex

Width 85u · Cap 100u

Flat chiseled summit apex. Tapered hairline left diagonal contrasted against a muscular right diagonal pillar. The low crossbar locks into the brand's horizon line.

04 / Clearspace Specification

Clearspace & Protected Exclusion Zone

To preserve visual authority and optical impact, the mark must be surrounded by an inviolable clear space. No other typographic, photographic, or graphic element may encroach within this boundary.

The "1X" Clearspace Metric

The fundamental clearspace unit X is defined as 100% of the Cap-Height of the STRATA wordmark (equivalent to the full vertical height of the initial 'S').

Perimeter Protection Rules

A minimum of 1X clear space must be maintained on all four sides (top, bottom, left, and right). For large-format display billboards, exterior architectural signs, and trade-fair booths, clearspace should be expanded to 1.5X.

Trim & Die-Cut Margins

When placing the mark on retail packaging, pouches, or print labels, the outer clearspace perimeter must never cross into the physical bag trim edge, heat-seal crimp line, or gusset folds.

05 / Minimum Size Rules

Scale Thresholds & Minimum Sizes

Strict minimum sizing ensures the delicate lapidary cuts, internal counter triangles, and fine serifs do not fill in or degrade under low resolution or ink bleed.

Wordmark (Digital)

24px Height / 100px Width
1:1 Actual Screen Scale (24px)

The smallest digital height for the standalone wordmark. Below 24px, the internal counter of the 'A' and the waist serifs on the 'R' begin to coalesce.

Full Lockup + Tagline

36px Height / 150px Width
1:1 Actual Screen Scale (36px)

When the secondary descriptor is included, minimum height increases to 36px to prevent the tagline tracking from becoming illegible.

Elevation Cipher (Favicon)

16px × 16px (16 CSS px)
16px & 32px Favicon / App Icon Scale

Below 24px, the wordmark must be substituted with the Elevation Cipher. Its bold geometric strata tiers retain instant identity recognition at 16×16px.

Physical Print Minimum

20 mm (0.78 in) Width
20 mm
Wordmark Print Floor
Offset Lithography & Gravure Packaging

For flexographic printing on kraft bags, minimum width is 24 mm. For foil stamping, blind embossing, or high-definition offset lithography, minimum width is 20 mm.

08 / Brand Integrity

Brand Violations & Unapproved Misuse

To safeguard the visual authority and optical integrity of STRATA, the following treatments are strictly prohibited across all print, physical, and digital media.

✕

Do Not Distort Aspect Ratio

Never stretch horizontally, compress vertically, or skew the glyphs. Scaling must always remain 100% proportional.

✕

Do Not Add Shadows or Gradients

Never apply decorative drop shadows, outer glows, 3D extrusions, or multi-color gradients. Keep forms flat and crisp.

✕

Do Not Alter Kerning or Baseline

Never reposition individual glyphs, alter optical kerning offsets, or shift letters off the established horizon datum line.

✕

Do Not Place on Clashing Backgrounds

Never place the wordmark on low-contrast muddy backgrounds, unapproved kraft textures, or busy photography without sufficient contrast.
