Frequently Asked Questions

Clear technical answers on ingest semantics, overages, burst handling, and enterprise migration.

How are ingestion overages calculated if our cluster experiences a traffic spike?
We never hard-drop spans or throttle ingestion during traffic spikes. On Team Production, if you exceed your 250M included monthly span allocation, additional spans are billed at a flat $0.12 per 1,000,000 spans ($0.00000012/span). You can also configure automated sampling triggers or dynamic head-based filtering rules to discard high-frequency healthcheck spans before ingestion.
What happens to traces after the hot NVMe retention window expires?
On Team and Enterprise plans, our background compactor engine converts expiring NVMe blocks into column-optimized Apache Parquet files and streams them directly into your customer-owned Amazon S3 or Google Cloud Storage bucket. Traces remain queryable via our DuckDB federated query engine at S3 latency (~1-3 seconds) with zero additional compute licensing fees.
Can we connect Kestrel to AWS via PrivateLink without routing over public transit?
Yes. AWS PrivateLink VPC Endpoint connections and GCP Private Service Connect are supported out of the box on Enterprise Fleet plans (and available as a $150/mo add-on for Team Production). Your telemetry traffic remains strictly within your cloud provider's backbone network, satisfying PCI-DSS and FedRAMP transit isolation standards.
Are there per-seat fees when our engineering team expands?
No. Kestrel has zero per-seat licensing. You can invite your entire engineering organization (5 engineers or 5,000 engineers) without incurring user seat taxes. We believe penalizing teams for granting engineers observability access creates dangerous blind spots. You pay only for node compute and indexed storage volume.
How does the 14-day production trial work?
The trial provisions a full Team Production tier cluster with 250M span headroom, dedicated OTLP brokers, and full eBPF collector support. No credit card is required to provision the initial staging broker. At the end of 14 days, you can choose annual or monthly billing or seamlessly scale down to the Developer free tier without losing configuration.
