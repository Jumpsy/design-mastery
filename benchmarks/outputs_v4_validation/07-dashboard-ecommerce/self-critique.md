1. **Dual-Axis Scaling Without Secondary Y-Axis Ticks**: While both the primary Revenue spline (amber) and the secondary Orders trajectory (cyan dashed) are plotted simultaneously on the main chart canvas, only the left-side Y-axis ($0k–$8.5k) provides explicit grid ticks and dollar labels. 2. **Keyboard Navigation Lacks Macro Steps**: While ArrowLeft and ArrowRight step smoothly across the 30 discrete date indices with live ARIA announcements, keyboard interaction lacks macro jump keys (PageUp/PageDown or Shift+Arrow for 7-day increments), which becomes slightly tedious when navigating across the 90-day view.
