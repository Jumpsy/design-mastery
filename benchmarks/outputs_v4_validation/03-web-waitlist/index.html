Acoustic Architecture

The science of unhurried conversation.

Standard voice assistants rely on rudimentary energy-based Voice Activity Detection (VAD). The moment you pause for four hundred milliseconds to formulate a word, the algorithm assumes you are done, sends your fragmented sentence to a cloud server, and interrupts you with a robotic hallucination.

Sotto replaces brittle silence counters with continuous prosodic evaluation. By analyzing intonation contours, pitch shifts, and respiratory intake patterns, Sotto distinguishes between a mid-sentence comma pause and a true terminal period. You can pace your room, mutter, backtrack, and formulate complex thoughts without feeling pressured by an impatient machine.
