Unit 3 // Ecology & Interdependence

The Architecture of Pond Inquiry

When young minds encounter an aquatic ecosystem, their initial instinct is to classify creatures as isolated nouns: "duck," "frog," "moss." The Wonderling framework moves the child from static nouns to dynamic systems.

Discussion Prompt for Parents:
"If the pond water gets murky from mud, which creature will notice first: the plant or the frog?"

Standards Alignment: Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS K-LS1-1 & 2-LS2-2).

Field Observation Guide

3 Steps for Outdoor Exploration

1. Look Before Touching: Encourage your child to kneel 2 feet back from the water's edge and stay silent for 30 seconds to observe water striders.

2. Surface Tension Experiment: Gently float a dry leaf versus a water-logged pebble. Ask what makes the surface act like a trampoline.

3. Sketch the Symmetry: Use the field notebook on page 28 to draw the five-point leaf structure of the white water lily.

05 // System Rules & Guidance

Iconography & Brand Integrity Rules

Icons share a consistent 2.0px optical stroke weight with rounded caps and joins on a 24x24 pixel grid. We maintain strict boundaries between inviting playfulness and infantilizing clutter.

DO: Playful, Structured & Humanist
  • Use tactile, super-elliptical containers that invite natural thumb interaction without sharp abrasive edges.
  • Anchor in warm oat & marine ink to preserve high ocular comfort and WCAG AAA compliance.
  • Reward curiosity through physical badges that celebrate real understanding rather than continuous slot-machine streaks.
  • Provide phonetic pronunciation chips that bridge acoustic hearing with printed letter recognition.
DON'T: Juvenile, Chaotic or Manipulative
  • Never use Comic Sans or faux-childish bubble fonts that impair emerging reading accuracy and patronize young learners.
  • Never clash uncalibrated saturated primaries (#FF0000, #00FF00, #0000FF) which trigger sensory fatigue.
  • Never employ guilt-based streak mechanics ("You broke Maya's streak!") or artificial daily scarcity timers.
  • Never insert full-screen advertisements or in-app microtransactions into a children's learning product.
