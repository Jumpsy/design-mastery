1. Biological Mechanism of Action

Formula N° 04 addresses the pathophysiology of compromised epidermal barrier integrity paired with follicular sebaceous gland hyperactivity. By supplying 10.0% ultra-purified Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) alongside 2.0% Zinc L-PCA, the formulation inhibits 5α-reductase enzymatic expression at the infundibulum without triggering compensatory xerosis.
