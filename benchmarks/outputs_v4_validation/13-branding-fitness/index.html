Clear-Space & Minimum Size 1.5X STEM RATIO

1.5X CLEAR MARGIN

Maintain a non-interference exclusion perimeter of 1.5× the base triangle width across all printed collateral and digital viewports. Minimum digital render width is 24px; minimum physical print size is 12mm.

Mandatory System Standards (Do)
  • Strictly maintain the 37.5° angle on the kinetic vector lever arm to reflect biomechanical torque.
  • Render all technical annotations and telemetry data in fixed-width tabular monospace typography.
  • Anchor physical products (cards, signs, hangtags) in tactile raw materials: brushed steel, PVD titanium, heavyweight twill.
  • Preserve high-contrast WCAG AAA legibility (minimum 7:1) across all digital member interfaces.
Prohibited Distortions (Don't)
  • Never apply soft decorative blurs, colorful gradients, or neon drop shadows to the master mark.
  • Never substitute generic fitness clipart, human silhouettes, or decorative dumbbells for biomechanical diagrams.
  • Never distort, skew, or rotate the base triangle away from the horizontal structural ground plane.
  • Never alter the color of the fulcrum pivot point away from Kinetic Vector Orange (#FF5500).
