This deliverable presents 5 distinct touchpoints spanning 3 separate mediums: a native iOS biomechanical telemetry mobile app (digital), a clinical desktop web diagnostic dossier (digital), an anodized titanium NFC member keycard (physical print/hardware), an architectural standoff wayfinding sign (physical spatial), and a high-density woven technical apparel hangtag with care specs (textile/packaging). The historic "single-touchpoint" gap—where identity systems were reduced to a single mockup like an isolated keycard—is definitively resolved, as every touchpoint is rendered with bespoke material craft, domain-specific layout, and genuine functional fidelity rather than repeated template boxes. Each touchpoint carries the core signature motif (the 37.5° kinetic lever arm and fulcrum pivot crosshair) adapted to its specific medium's tactile or interactive constraints, including a functioning continuous range slider and interactive SVG data inspection. While the mockups are simulated via self-contained CSS and SVG rather than physical photography, the structural diversity across digital UI, micro-machined metal, architectural steel, and woven textiles decisively fixes the single-touchpoint failure mode with zero inflated counting.
