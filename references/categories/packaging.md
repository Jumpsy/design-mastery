# Packaging

**Panel priority**: front panel gets brand name + 1 product descriptor + net
weight/volume — nothing else competes for primary attention. Secondary info
(ingredients, lot codes, barcodes) moves to side/back panels or a small corner
treatment.

**Material honesty**: design should acknowledge the substrate (matte kraft vs. glossy
tin vs. glass label) — a flat-color digital mockup that ignores material reads as
generic; note the intended material even in a flat digital comp.

**Restraint**: one structural device (a hairline border, a single accent-color rule) is
enough; packaging slop is usually over-decoration (multiple badges, competing seals,
gradient bursts), not under-decoration.

**Anti-patterns**: generic circular badge/seal logo, bright unearned gradient burst,
multiple typefaces competing on one small panel, fake "artisanal" texture overlays
applied indiscriminately.

**Verified build**: `packaging-demo/label.svg` — coffee-bag label, near-black ground,
single hairline gold border, italic serif wordmark (deliberate transfer of the web-hero
"serif+italic" pattern into a physical-product context), one lot-number roundel as the
sole decorative flourish. Rendered and confirmed at 360×480.
