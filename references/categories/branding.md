# Branding / identity systems

**Core deliverable set**: 1 primary mark + 1 wordmark lockup, a 3–5 color palette with
named semantic roles (not just hex swatches), a 2-typeface system (display + body),
and explicit min-size/clearspace/don't rules for the mark.

**Palette construction**: 1 dominant neutral (bg), 1 ink (text), 1 signature accent used
sparingly and consistently across every touchpoint (this repetition — not the hue
itself — is what makes a brand color feel "owned"; see Anthropic's coral, Stripe's
gradient signature, Linear's near-black).

**Type pairing**: pick display+body from converging real-brand data in `design-md/` —
serif display + sans body reads warm/editorial (Claude, StudyFetch-adjacent); sans-only
with tight tracking reads technical (Linear, Vercel); avoid pairing two similar-weight
sans faces (no visual contrast = looks accidental, not chosen).

**Anti-patterns**: generic "abstract swoosh" or gradient-blob logo with no conceptual
tie to the brief, more than one accent color competing for attention, palette chosen
from a trend board instead of the brand's actual positioning.

**Verified build**: `brand-guidelines-demo/sheet.html` — a one-page spec sheet showing
the packaging-demo "Fieldnote" mark, palette swatches with role labels, type scale
sample, and clearspace diagram. Rendered and confirmed.
