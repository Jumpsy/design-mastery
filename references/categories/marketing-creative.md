# Marketing creative (social/ad banners)

**Format discipline**: design to the actual placement's aspect ratio (1:1 feed, 4:5
feed-tall, 9:16 story/reel) — a scaled-down landing-page hero cropped to fit is the most
common failure mode, not a from-scratch composition for the format.

**Message hierarchy**: one claim, one supporting proof point, one CTA — legible at
thumbnail scale (assume the viewer sees it at ~120px wide while scrolling). Type sized
so the primary claim is readable at that scale, not just at full resolution.

**Brand consistency under compression**: reuse the exact palette/type system from the
brand's other surfaces (web, product) rather than inventing ad-only styling — inconsistent
ad creative is a top "this feels like a scam/AI" signal for users.

**Anti-patterns**: text crammed edge-to-edge with no margin, more than one CTA
competing, stock photography disconnected from the actual product, urgency-bait styling
(flashing "SALE" badges, countdown-timer graphics) unless the brand's tone specifically
calls for it.

**Verified build**: `marketing-creative-demo/ad.html` at 1080×1080 (1:1 feed format) —
reuses the packaging-demo's Fieldnote brand system (palette, italic-serif wordmark) for
a promotional post, single claim + single CTA, rendered and confirmed.
